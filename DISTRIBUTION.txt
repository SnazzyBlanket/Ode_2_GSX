macOS: Apple Silicon arm64, macOS 13 Ventura or later (including 14 Sonoma).
Unzip and move ODE TO GSX.app to your Applications folder. This preview is
ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may require an
explicit Open Anyway approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
Workplace device policies may disallow unsigned third-party software.
VST3: copy the complete .vst3 bundle to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/.
AU: copy the .component bundle to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/.

Windows: x64. Unzip the standalone and run ODE TO GSX.exe.
VST3: copy the complete ODE TO GSX.vst3 directory to
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ and rescan plugins in your DAW.
These preview binaries are not certificate-signed; Windows may show a warning.

Plugin use: insert as an instrument on an audio/instrument track and press
Start in its editor. This is a free-running generator; MIDI notes and DAW
transport do not trigger it. State is saved with the DAW session. Host
parameter automation is not yet exposed. Standalone audio device settings
are in the JUCE wrapper's Options menu. Start with a low output level.
